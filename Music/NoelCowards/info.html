British Songs: Noel Cowards!

The following slides were made by Alice Aung

Noel Cowards
  • Born in 1899 to lower middle class family
  • A singer and dancer. Was sent by Winston to sing to troops and perform for entertainment.
  • Really wanted to become rich and successful and achieved this during the wars with his humorous plays and songs.
  • Was the top of Nazi’s death list (Fun Fact!)
Don’t Let’s Be Beastly to the Germans
  • Written to critique people that were too forgiving to the Germans during WW2.
  • Was a song against the nazis that blended both humor and British patriotism.
  • Actually was Winston Churchill’s favorite song!
  • Banned in 1943 because survivors didn’t like the humor intended in it. Found it disrespectful and thought it was Pro-German.
