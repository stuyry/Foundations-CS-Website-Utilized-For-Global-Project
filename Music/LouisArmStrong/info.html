Introduction
Louis Armstrong was an extremely popular African American Blues musician at the time, he greatly helped boost the morales of many young soldiers fighting during a war that they themselves did not fully understand. With his soothing rhythms and positive upbeats, he was able to give many people serving in the war a more positive outlook on the big battle that they were fighting, although things were not always the best in the front lines, and even the back.
Having already amassed a great amount of popularity by ww2, Louis Armstrong appeared on radio broadcasts, notably Jubilee, playing some of his greatest (as shown on the right) which aimed to play optimistic and soul-lifting music for all the people who were working for and were affected by the war. In times where there were no televisions to go around, radio was a source of escape and refuge, and even a reminder of loved ones back home, from the crumbling world around the people caught in the World War.
