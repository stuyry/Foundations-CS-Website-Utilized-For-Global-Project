British Songs: Vera Lynn!

The following slides were made by Alice Aung

Vera Lynn
  • Living from 1917-2020 (103 years old), she was an English singer who was nicknamed “the Forces’ Sweetheart” due to her songs of love and longing resonating with the members of the military.
  • Sang for people hiding in london tube stations during air raids.
  • Had a weekly radio show called Sincerely Yours where she sang requested songs from British soldiers.
  • Gave concerts to the British soldiers in India, Burma, and Egypt.
There’ll Always Be an England
  • It’s lyrics provided comfort to those that were separated from their loved ones. For example, “We'll meet again / Don't know where…Keep smiling through / Just like you always do”
  • It was first recorded during the phoney war where there was still minimal fighting and mainly recruition.
  • The performance was what got people. Low tone and emotional delivery.
  • Received backlash as people assumed it was affecting the military’s morales which resulted in the multiple defeats in northern Africa.
