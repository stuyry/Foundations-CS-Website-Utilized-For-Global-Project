British Songs: Gracie Fields!

The following slides were made by Alice Aung

Gracie Fields
  • Born in 1898, her career started in traveling British troops while earning extra money from factory work.
  • Became an actress but once World War 2 erupted, she entertained british troops stationed in France while falling ill.
  • Her husband was Italian and during that time they were deemed aliens so she received backlash and moved to North America for concerts. Received even more backlash but eventually was forgiven when she was raising money for Europe’s war efforts from it.
Wish Me Luck As You Wave Me Goodbye
  • The upbeat music and peppy delivery sparked optimism instead of sorrow when being departed for the war.
  • Helped keep soldiers calm and motivated them to carry on.
  • “Wish me luck as you wave me goodbye / Not a tear, but a cheer, / make it gay / Give me a smile I can keep all the while / In my heart while I’m away”
