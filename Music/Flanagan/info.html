British Songs: Flanagan and Allen!

The following slides were made by Alice Aung

Flanagan and Allen
  • Comedic singing duo against Germany.
  • Many of their songs poked at Hitler and German troops and were sing-alongs. Some were romantic to comfort the ones who were in absence of a loved one due to their departure for the military.
  • They both met in World War I while fighting in the British troops but didn’t start working together until six years after the war ended.
  • Fun Fact : Flanagan ran away to sea at the age of 14!
Run Rabbit Run
  • The original lyrics were about a farmer shooting rabbits to make pie but then it was changed to mock Hitler and then revamped to “Run Adolf Run!”
  • Germans sent bombs that were targeted towards British ships but instead it created a massive crater which allegedly killed two bunnies. After this, the lyrics were taken into interpretation and was used as a mockery of German’s clumsiness.
  • Became a rallying call
  • Used strange and funny language like the repetition of run rabbit run.
