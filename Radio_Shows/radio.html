Radio Shows

Les Français parlent aux Français
  • (The French Speak to the French) - A plaque commemorating the radio show at the Cemetery of Asnelles
  • Ici Londres ! Les Français parlent aux Français…” (“This is London! The French speaking to the French…”)
  • Broadcasted by the BBC from the first members of resistance who escaped Germany-occupied France, this radio show was the voice of the Free French Forces under leader Charles de Gaulle. In these episodes he called for the rise of French resistance against the Germans, countering the Nazi propaganda of Radio France and Radio Vichy.
Soldiers of the Press
  • An advertisement for the radio show in the magazine Broadcasting
  • On the American front, this weekly radio series dramatized the most recent breaking news and feature stories on both fronts of the war in first person, but narrated by famous radio actors. Each episode was written with patriotic, nationalistic language, meant to lift the spirit of the American people.
  • As seen in the music and the sheet music, Lots of swing melodies are present, utilizing the repetition of eighth rests and tied eighth notes in the runs that follow, they are also able to create a very jazzy and catchy tune, mutual to the many ‘propaganda’ songs that developed during the WW2 period.
