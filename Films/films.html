British Films
A short 1940’s British Documentary that uses realism & documentary footage
For your notes!
Depicts civilians going about their normal lives during the day & witnessing destruction at night
Emphasizes resilience & determination; strong example of allied propaganda
Highlights courage & unity of ordinary people which built strength + endurance
This film was distributed to the US to influence American public opinion and build support for Britain in WWII. It portrayed the British in such a raw, human way, that it gained much sympathy and eventual involvement from America

American Films
An animated short film by Walt Disney, and a Clear representation of WWII propaganda designed to influence public perception
For your notes!
Exhaggerates harshness & absurdity of life in Nazi Germany, using satire & humor makes message more accessive & memorable while still reinforicing fear of authoritarianism
Aims to boost American moral & patriotism - encouraging viewers to appreciate life in America
Donald Duck experiences a nightmare in which he is a citizen of Nazi Germany and is surrounded with never ending propaganda- swastikas appear everywhere, even in ordinary objects
He is forced into exhausting manual labor in a weapons factory, while consistently saluting to Adolf Hitler. This oppressive environment ultimately sends Donald into a mental breakdown
He wakes up relieved to discover that he is an American citizen, grateful for his freedom
